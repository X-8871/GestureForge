7.5 Expansion Port J11
The expansion port J11 is also a 40-pin 2.54mm dual-row connector, allowing users to expand
with more peripherals and interfaces. Currently, ALINX provides modules such as: ADDA modules, LCD
screen modules, Gigabit Ethernet modules, audio input/output modules, matrix keyboard modules, and 500W
binocular vision camera modules . The expansion port includes one 5V power supply, two 3.3V power
supply, three ground lines, and 34 I/O ports. The I/O signals are connected to the ZYNQ PL's
BANK35, with a default voltage level of 3.3V. All I/O signals on expansion port J11 can be changed
by replacing the power supply chip ( SPX3819M5-3-3 ) on the development board. Do not connect
directly to 5V devices to avoid damaging the FPGA. If you need to connect 5V devices, you need to use a level
converter chip.
A 33-ohm resistor array is connected in series between the expansion port and the FPGA to
protect the FPGA from damage caused by excessive external voltage or current. The P and N traces
on the PCB are routed differentially, with the differential impedance controlled at 100 ohms. The pin
assignments for the J11 expansion port are shown in the table below:
