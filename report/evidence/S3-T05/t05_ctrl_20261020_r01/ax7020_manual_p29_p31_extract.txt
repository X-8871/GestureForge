7.4 Expansion Port J10
Expansion port J10 is a 40-pin 2.54mm dual-row connector, providing users with more
peripherals and interfaces. Currently, ALINX offers modules including: ADDA modules, LCD screen
modules, Gigabit Ethernet modules, audio input/output modules, matrix keyboard modules, and 500W binocular
vision camera modules . The expansion port includes one 5V power supply, two 3.3V power supply,
three ground lines, and 34 I/O ports. The I/O signals connect to BANK34 and BANK35 on the ZYNQ
PL, with a default voltage level of 3.3V. Some I/O signals on expansion port J10 can have their
voltage levels changed by replacing the power supply chip ( SPX3819M5-3-3 ) on the development
board. Do not connect directly to 5V devices to avoid damaging the FPGA. If you need to connect 5V devices,
you need to use a level converter chip.
A 33-ohm resistor array is connected in series between the expansion port and the FPGA to
protect the FPGA from damage caused by excessive external voltage or current. The P and N traces
on the PCB design use differential routing, controlling the differential impedance to 100 ohms. The
pin assignments for the J10 expansion port are shown in the table below:

7.6 User LED
The PL section of the AX7020 development board connects to four LEDs. The LED signals are
connected to the BANK35 I/O pin of the PL section. When the BANK35 I/O pin outputs logic 0, the
LED is lit; when it outputs logic 1, the LED is off.
